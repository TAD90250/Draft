2026 Redraft Cheat Sheet — 12-Team PPR

Consensus-based rankings (CBS Fantasy Football Today, PFF, FantasyPros) as of late Aug 2026 preseason. Verify injury news day-of.
Proj Pts = blended half-PPR points/game estimate, now populated for the top 80 of each sheet. Three sourcing tiers, clearly different in rigor: (1) ~52 players use Sleeper's actual 2026 season projections (via 4for4.com's live Sleeper feed), computed from Sleeper's own projected stat lines; (2) Rashee Rice, Malik Nabers, Nico Collins, Josh Jacobs use earlier non-Sleeper estimates, and Derrick Henry blends his real 2025 rushing line with an estimated receiving total; (3) roughly 40 remaining players (mostly WR2/RB2-tier and backup QBs not visible in Sleeper's top-10-per-position) use Claude's own ballpark estimate based on 2026 ADP/consensus-ranking context gathered in this conversation — not tied to any specific projection system, and meaningfully less rigorous than tiers 1-2. Ricky Pearsall has no value — season-ending injury per camp reports. OL = team offensive line rank (PFF, 1=best). QB = team's starting QB rank league-wide (1=best), a consensus-informed estimate — not a standalone QB cheat sheet. SOS = team's rest-of-season schedule favorability, 0-100 (100=easiest), sourced live from RotoWire (QB/team-level view applied as a proxy across positions — treat as directional). Camp Buzz = real, sourced training-camp news as of late Aug 2026 for players camp reporters actually covered; "No notable camp buzz reported" means exactly that. Green = favorable/top third, yellow = middle third, red = unfavorable/bottom third for OL/QB/SOS. Team tags reflect confirmed 2026 offseason trades/free agency as of late Aug 2026; Tyreek Hill is tagged FA. A few deep-bench backfield free agents (Nick Chubb, Najee Harris, Joe Mixon) were unsigned as of the last transaction check — verify before draft day. Check the box next to a player once they're drafted (by anyone) to hide them from the board; use Undo if you check the wrong one, or Reset All to start a new draft. Your picks and any manual reordering save automatically in this browser; use "Save as file" to download a copy with the current state baked in if you want to share it or move it to another device.
